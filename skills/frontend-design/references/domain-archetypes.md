# Domain Archetypes & Visual Directions

When designing for a specific industry, align with its visual vernacular while taking one bold, justifiable design choice.

| Domain Category | Core Identity & Atmosphere | Color Token Strategy | Typography Stack | Signature Layout Element |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Corporate & Enterprise** *(Web Perusahaan / Holding)* | Institutional authority, global footprint, executive prestige, strategic clarity. | Enterprise navy (`#0A2540`), deep slate (`#0F172A`), royal blue or gold accent (`#2563EB` / `#C5A059`), crisp off-white surface (`#F8FAFC`). | Display: *Playfair Display* / *Clash Display* <br> Body: *DM Sans* / *Figtree* <br> Accent: *Cinzel* / *Cormorant* | Multi-column key impact metrics bar, staggered editorial executive statement, split investor/ESG highlights. |
| **Fintech & Modern Finance** | Precision, trust, high data density, real-time feedback. | Deep navy (`#0B132B`), slate (`#1C2541`), emerald green accent (`#10B981`), icy white background (`#F8FAFC`). | Display: *Instrument Serif* / *Cinzel* <br> Body: *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Switzer* <br> Mono: *JetBrains Mono* (for figures) | Live ticker bar, tabular data alignment (`tabular-nums`), high-contrast subtle borders (`border-slate-800/10`). |
| **B2B / B2C SaaS** | Structured efficiency, workflow velocity, modularity, clarity. | Muted cobalt (`#1E40AF`), neutral zinc (`#18181B`), surface (`#FAFAFA`), subtle cyan hover state (`#06B6D4`). | Display: *Sora* / *Outfit* <br> Body: *DM Sans* / *Figtree* | Modular command center, split view pane, shortcut badge overlays (`Kbd` keys). |
| **Web & Mobile Applications** | Native feel, spatial hierarchy, touch-target responsiveness. | Deep obsidian (`#09090B`), elevated card surfaces (`#18181B`), crisp brand pop (`#6366F1`). | Display: *SF Pro Display* / *Cabinet Grotesk* <br> Body: *SF Pro Text* / *Figtree* | Floating bottom navigation/dock, glassy blurred backdrop headers (`backdrop-blur-md`). |
| **Developer Tools & AI Infra** | Extreme code/data density, utility, terminal aesthetic, low friction. | Pitch black (`#050505`), terminal dark card (`#0F0F11`), amber or violet syntax highlight (`#F59E0B` / `#8B5CF6`). | Display: *Geist* / *Space Grotesk* <br> Body: *Figtree* / *Switzer* <br> Code: *Fira Code* / *Geist Mono* | Monospace command palette (`Cmd+K`), interactive code diff, grid line overlay backgrounds. |
| **E-Commerce & DTC** | Tactile, editorial, immersive storytelling, high contrast. | Warm ivory (`#FAFAFA`), editorial black (`#111111`), warm clay or olive accent (`#8C6D58`). | Display: *Playfair Display* / *Clash Display* <br> Body: *Satoshi* / *Switzer* | Large full-bleed image hero, grid hover zoom, sticky add-to-bag footer. |
| **HealthTech & Bio Sciences** | Calming clarity, clinical precision, human warmth, high legibility. | Clean teal (`#0D9488`), soft mint surface (`#F0FDF4`), clinical charcoal (`#1E293B`). | Display: *Outfit* / *Albert Sans* <br> Body: *Readex Pro* / *Figtree* | Health metric sparklines, status indicators with glowing pulses. |
| **Pendidikan & EdTech** *(Education & Academia)* | Inspiring, accessible, structured progression, academic rigor. | Academic deep blue (`#1E3A8A`), vibrant indigo/orange accent (`#4F46E5` / `#EA580C`), clean white background (`#FFFFFF`). | Display: *Lexend* / *Merriweather* <br> Body: *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Open Sans* | Course progress stepper, interactive syllabus tree, student achievement counters. |
| **Pemerintahan & Layanan Publik** *(GovTech / Public Sector)* | High legibility, neutral trustworthiness, universal accessibility, civic clarity. | Official red/white or civic blue (`#0284C7`), dark neutral text (`#0F172A`), high contrast light gray background (`#F1F5F9`). | Display: *Public Sans* / *Source Sans Pro* <br> Body: *Figtree* / *Readex Pro* | Emergency alert notice bar, step-by-step public service wizard, multi-language quick switcher. |
| **Nirlaba & Yayasan** *(Non-Profit & NGO)* | Human-centric impact, emotional resonance, transparency, urgent call-to-action. | Forest green (`#15803D`), earthy warm sand (`#FFEFDB`), terracotta or gold warm accent (`#C2410C`). | Display: *Fraunces* / *Lora* <br> Body: *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Lato* | Impact ticker bar (funds raised vs target), full-width photo narrative banner, quick donation widget. |
| **Media, Berita & Penerbitan** *(Editorial & Media)* | Information density, typographic authority, rapid scannability, editorial rhythm. | Newspaper ink black (`#121212`), newsprint off-white (`#F9F9FB`), breaking news crimson accent (`#DC2626`). | Display: *Newsreader* / *Bodoni Moda* <br> Body: *Source Serif Pro* / *Figtree* | Asymmetric headline grid, reading time & progress bar indicator, paywall / newsletter ribbon. |
